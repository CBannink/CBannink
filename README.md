### 🏠 Currently building [HomeScout.io](https://homescout.io)

**AI-powered rental search for Dublin** — because finding a home here shouldn't feel like a second job.

- **Natural-language search** — describe your dream place in plain English, HomeScout finds it
- **24/7 Match Alerts** — AI hunts new listings while you sleep and emails you when one fits
- **AI lease review** — upload a contract, get unusual clauses flagged before you sign
- **Market Scout** — interactive map of 28 Dublin postal districts with real rent data
- **Viewing scheduler, AI email drafts, commute calculator** — the whole rental stack in one place

Built for expats, students, professionals and anyone tired of refreshing Daft at 3am.
